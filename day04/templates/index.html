About this analysis

ANOVA compares average measurements to test whether group, treatment, or their interaction is associated with differences. It assumes independent observations, approximately normal residuals, and similar variance across combinations. Partial eta-squared is an ANOVA effect size. This program is for independent observations, not repeated measurements on the same subjects.
